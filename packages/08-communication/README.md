# Communication Skills
**Package:** `@mad-scientist/communication` | **1 Skill** | Version 1.0.0

---

## What This Package Does For You

This package connects your AI agent to Discord — the chat platform many communities use to stay connected. Your AI can read messages in your Discord server, post updates, welcome new members, and relay information between your team channels.

Think of it as a community manager that never sleeps, never forgets to post, and can answer common questions even at 3am.

---

## Skills In This Package

### 1. Discord Bot Control
**What it does:** Connects your AI agent to your Discord server as a bot. It can read messages in channels, post new messages, respond to commands, welcome new members, and relay information from your other AI agents into Discord.

**What to say:**  
> "Post an announcement in our #deals channel that we're now buying houses in Anne Arundel County"
> "Welcome any new members who join the server with a message about our next meetup"
> "When someone posts in #questions with the word 'wholesale,' reply with our contact info"

**What you get:** Your AI agent active in Discord, posting, responding, and managing community interactions on your behalf.

---

## What You Can Set Up

**Automated Welcome Messages**  
New members get a personal welcome message when they join, with information about your community and what to do next.

**Deal Alerts**  
When your AI finds a new property deal, it posts automatically to your Discord server so your investors see it instantly.

**Question Auto-Response**  
Common questions get instant answers — without you having to repeat yourself at 2am.

**Content Cross-Posting**  
When you post on YouTube or social media, your AI announces it in Discord automatically.

**Team Coordination**  
Your different AI agents (Bob, Sarah, etc.) can post status updates directly to specific Discord channels so your whole team sees what's happening.

## How To Use This Package

### Installation
```bash
npm install @mad-scientist/communication
```

Or symlink into your Hermes skills folder:
```bash
ln -s $(pwd)/skills ~/.hermes/skills/communication
```

### Quick Start
```
skill_view(name="discord")
```

Then tell your AI agent:
> "Connect me to my Discord server at [server invite link]"
