# AI Agent Skills
**Package:** `@mad-scientist/ai-agents` | **18 Skills** | Version 1.0.0

---

## What This Package Does For You

This package is your virtual team of specialized AI workers. You already have Sarah (your outbound caller), Bob (your operations manager), and Tammie (your HR assistant). This package makes them run and lets you build new AI team members for any job you need.

These skills let you spin up specialized AI agents — one to review code, another to manage your OpenClaw dashboard, another to run automated hiring, another to call leads. Each agent is purpose-built for its role and works independently.

---

## Skills In This Package

### 1. Bob (Operations Agent)
**What it does:** Your general-purpose operations manager. Handles skip-tracing, list management, property research, data processing, and any task that involves working with property lists and contact information.

**What to say:**  
> "Bob, I need to skip-trace these 20 addresses and find the owner's phone number for each one"

**What you get:** A spreadsheet with owner name, phone number, and mailing address for all 20 properties.

---

### 2. Sarah (Outbound Caller)
**What it does:** Your AI voice caller. Calls leads on the phone, speaks to them naturally using a realistic AI voice, asks pre-set questions, and reports back what was said. Uses VAPI (Voice AI Platform).

**What to say:**  
> "Sarah, call this phone number 410-555-1234 and ask if they're interested in selling their property for cash"

**What you get:** A full call transcript and summary of the conversation, plus a recommendation on next steps.

---

### 3. HR Hiring (Tammie)
**What it does:** Runs your hiring workflow on Upwork — posts jobs, screens candidates, and ranks applicants by fit. Saves hours of reviewing resumes and interviewing unqualified people.

**What to say:**  
> "Tammie, post a job on Upwork for a virtual assistant and rank the top 5 candidates by experience"

**What you get:** Job posted to Upwork, top candidates ranked and summarized, ready for you to interview.

---

### 4. OpenClaw Agent Creation
**What it does:** Creates new AI agent personas from scratch — defines their personality, capabilities, tools, and workflow. Use this to build specialized agents for specific business functions.

**What to say:**  
> "Create me a new AI agent called 'Rehab Inspector' that reviews property photos and estimates repair costs"

**What you get:** A new agent persona created and ready to use, with its own workspace and configuration.

---

### 5. OpenClaw Agent Browser
**What it does:** Lets your AI agent control a web browser — clicks buttons, fills forms, navigates pages — like a robot that can do anything a human can do on a website.

**What to say:**  
> "Go to the county assessor website, search for property 123 Main St, and tell me the last sale price"

**What you get:** The information extracted from the website, delivered to you as if your AI agent read it with its own eyes.

---

### 6. OpenClaw Docker Migration
**What it does:** Moves your OpenClaw installation between servers or restructures it within Docker. Used when you need to move your AI platform to a new server without rebuilding from scratch.

**What to say:**  
> "Migrate my OpenClaw setup from my old VPS to my new server"

**What you get:** Complete OpenClaw installation on your new server, identical to the old one, with all agents and settings preserved.

---

### 7. OpenClaw Logo Maker
**What it does:** Generates logos and brand assets for your AI agents so each persona has a visual identity. Gives each agent a professional avatar that fits your brand.

**What to say:**  
> "Make a logo for my AI assistant 'Bob' — professional, clean, with a data/operations feel"

**What you get:** A logo image file and suggested avatar for your agent, ready to use in dashboards and profiles.

---

### 8. OpenClaw Access Troubleshooting
**What it does:** Diagnoses and fixes connection issues with your OpenClaw platform — when agents go offline, connections drop, or the dashboard stops loading.

**What to say:**  
> "My OpenClaw dashboard says disconnected — fix it"

**What you get:** Problem diagnosed and fixed, or clear instructions on what to do next.

---

### 9. Node Connect
**What it does:** Diagnoses why a specific OpenClaw node (AI agent) isn't connecting properly to the system. Finds the exact configuration issue and repairs it.

**What to say:**  
> "My agent 'Sarah' shows as offline but she was working yesterday — diagnose why"

**What you get:** Root cause identified (wrong API key, network issue, config error) and fixed or instructions provided.

---

### 10. Agent Browser
**What it does:** A dedicated browser automation tool specifically for AI agents — lets them navigate the web, fill out forms, extract data, and interact with web applications. Used when you need your agent to do something on a website.

**What to say:**  
> "Have my agent look up the property records on the county website for these 5 addresses and compile the results"

**What you get:** Structured data from the county website for all 5 addresses, in a spreadsheet-ready format.

---

### 11. Autonomous AI Agents
**What it does:** Spawns fully autonomous AI agents that work independently on a task without needing instruction — starts a job, decides the next steps, and reports back when done. Use for tasks that are too complex for a single command.

**What to say:**  
> "Spin up an autonomous agent to research the top 20 real estate investing podcasts and summarize their key topics and listener counts"

**What you get:** The agent works independently and reports back with a complete research summary when finished.

---

### 12. HR (Human Resources General)
**What it does:** A general HR assistant for managing your team, onboarding new contractors, tracking roles, and organizing your human resources workflows.

**What to say:**  
> "Set up an onboarding process for new virtual assistants I hire from Upwork"

**What you get:** A complete onboarding checklist, template welcome email, and task assignment structure for new hires.

---

### 13. Beautiful Websites
**What it does:** Finds local businesses with outdated, ugly, or broken websites and generates a modern redesign proposal. Used for lead generation or for offering web design services.

**What to say:**  
> "Find 20 local businesses in Baltimore with outdated websites and generate redesign proposals for each"

**What you get:** A list of 20 businesses with their current website URLs and AI-generated redesign proposals ready to send.

---

### 14. Enhancor
**What it does:** An AI agent specifically designed to enhance and improve existing content, code, documents, or workflows. Reviews what you have and suggests (and implements) improvements.

**What to say:**  
> "Enhancor, review my email sequence for my We Buy Houses campaign and tell me what's wrong with it"

**What you get:** A detailed critique and a revised version of your email sequence with improvements applied.

---

### 15. Pikastream Video Meeting
**What it does:** Joins your Google Meet or Zoom call as an AI participant, takes notes, transcribes the meeting, and produces a summary and action items afterward.

**What to say:**  
> "Join my 2pm sales call on Google Meet and give me a summary of what was discussed and agreed"

**What you get:** Full meeting transcript, summary with key points, and a list of action items assigned to each person.

---

### 16. REI AI Zoom Processor
**What it does:** Processes Zoom (or any video) transcripts specifically for real estate investing workflows — turns raw meeting transcripts into deal analysis, lead follow-up notes, and investor updates.

**What to say:**  
> "Process this Zoom recording of my investor meeting and create follow-up tasks for each deal discussed"

**What you get:** Meeting summary with deal-specific action items, owner contact tasks, and financing next steps.

---

### 17. OpenAI Whisper API
**What it does:** Transcribes audio files (recordings, voice memos, podcast episodes) to text using OpenAI's Whisper model — highly accurate, handles multiple speakers, works in many languages.

**What to say:**  
> "Transcribe this audio file of my property walkthrough inspection"

**What you get:** A full text transcript of the audio with speaker labels.

---

### 18. MCPorter
**What it does:** Configures and manages the Model Context Protocol (MCP) — the system that connects your AI agents to external tools and data sources. Sets up new tool connections and authenticates them.

**What to say:**  
> "Set up an MCP connection to my Google Drive so my AI agents can read my spreadsheets"

**What you get:** MCP configured and connected, your AI agent now has access to Google Drive files.

## How To Use This Package

### Installation
```bash
npm install @mad-scientist/ai-agents
```

Or symlink into your Hermes skills folder:
```bash
ln -s $(pwd)/skills ~/.hermes/skills/ai-agents
```

### Quick Start
```
skill_view(name="bob")
skill_view(name="sarah-outbound-caller")
skill_view(name="hr-hiring")
skill_view(name="openclaw-agent-creation")
```
