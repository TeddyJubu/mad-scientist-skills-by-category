# Image & Graphics Skills
**Package:** `@mad-scientist/image-graphics` | **8 Skills** | Version 1.0.0

---

## What This Package Does For You

This package puts a full creative studio inside your AI agent. You can generate any image from a plain-English description, create AI avatar videos where you appear on screen talking without filming anything, build animated videos, and edit existing photos by describing what you want changed.

No design skills, no expensive software subscriptions, no waiting on a graphic designer. You describe what you want and it creates it in seconds.

---

## Skills In This Package

### 1. GPT Image 2 (Image Generation)
**What it does:** The flagship image generator — creates any image from a text description. Logos, product photos, ad creatives, illustrations, concept art, social media graphics, home photos retouched to look better. Just describe it.

**What to say:**  
> "Create a photo-realistic image of a beautiful renovated kitchen with white cabinets, marble countertops, and a large island"

**What you get:** A high-resolution image file, ready to use on your website, social media, or ads.

---

### 2. HeyGen Avatar Video
**What it does:** Creates videos where an AI-generated avatar appears on screen and speaks. Uses your own face (upload a photo) so you can make videos of yourself saying anything — without filming. Perfect for personalized video messages at scale.

**What to say:**  
> "Make a HeyGen video of me saying 'Thanks for your offer — let me review it and get back to you within 24 hours'"

**What you get:** A video file of your avatar speaking those words, downloadable and shareable.

---

### 3. Remotion (Animated Videos)
**What it does:** Creates animated, data-driven videos — think motion graphics with charts, animated explainers, or stylized video content. Used for video content that needs to look polished and professional.

**What to say:**  
> "Create an animated video showing the growth of my real estate portfolio over 3 years with a bar chart animation"

**What you get:** A polished animated MP4 video file, ready to post to YouTube or social media.

---

### 4. Remotion Video Editing
**What it does:** Edits existing videos using Remotion's programmable video framework — adds captions, overlays, animated text, split screens, and other effects to raw footage.

**What to say:**  
> "Add animated captions and a logo intro to this webinar recording"

**What you get:** The edited video file with captions, logo, and intro baked in.

---

### 5. Gemini Image Editor (Edit Existing Images)
**What it does:** Edits photos you already have — removes objects, changes backgrounds, retouches faces, adds or replaces elements. Upload a photo and describe the change in plain English.

**What to say:**  
> "Take this photo of my house and remove the cars in the driveway, then make the grass look greener"

**What you get:** The edited photo with the changes made, downloadable as a new image file.

---

### 6. Nano Banana Image Generation
**What it does:** An alternative image generator using Google's Imagen model. Good for photorealistic images, artistic styles, and content that requires Google's specific visual training.

**What to say:**  
> "Generate an artistic, stylized illustration of a 'We Buy Houses' sign in front of a row of townhouses"

**What you get:** An image file in the style specified, ready to use.

---

### 7. Nano Banana Pro
**What it does:** The higher-quality version of Nano Banana with more detail, better composition, and support for larger image sizes. For final, client-facing graphics that need to look polished.

**What to say:**  
> "Create a polished hero image for my 'How to Wholesale Real Estate' landing page — professional, clean, real estate themed"

**What you get:** A high-resolution, publication-ready image file.

---

### 8. Veed Fabtoc (Talking Head Video)
**What it does:** Creates talking head videos from a single photo — takes a headshot, animates it to look like the person is speaking naturally, and adds a voiceover. For creating personal video content at scale without filming.

**What to say:**  
> "Make a talking head video from my headshot with the voiceover: 'If you're watching this, you're pre-approved for our cash offer program'"

**What you get:** A video file with your animated avatar speaking the script, downloadable and shareable.

## How To Use This Package

### Installation
```bash
npm install @mad-scientist/image-graphics
```

Or symlink into your Hermes skills folder:
```bash
ln -s $(pwd)/skills ~/.hermes/skills/image-graphics
```

### Quick Start
```
skill_view(name="gpt-image-2")
skill_view(name="heygen-avatar-video")
skill_view(name="gemini-image-editor")
```
